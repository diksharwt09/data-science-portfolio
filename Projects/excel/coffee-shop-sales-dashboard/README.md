# Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing sales performance across a 3-location coffee shop chain, covering revenue by store, product category, time of day, day of week, and monthly trend.

## Dataset
Coffee Shop Sales transaction data — ~149,000 individual transactions across 3 store locations (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Lower Manhattan), covering January-June.

Columns include: transaction date/time, store location, product category, product type, product detail, transaction quantity, and unit price.

## What I Built
- **Pivot tables** summarizing revenue by store location, product category/type, hour of day, day of week, and month
- **KPI cards**: Total Revenue, Total Transactions, Average Order Value
- **Dashboard visuals**: bar charts (revenue by category, by store location, top 5 products by revenue and by quantity), a line chart (revenue by hour of day), and a column chart (revenue by day of week)
- **Slicers** for store location and product category/type/detail to filter the dashboard interactively
- **Navigation buttons** linking to each underlying analysis sheet (Revenue by Store, Revenue by Category, Busiest Hour, Day of Week Trend, Top 5 by Revenue, Top 5 by Quantity, Data)

## Key Insights
- **Total revenue was $698,812.33** across 149,116 transactions, for an average order value of **$4.69**
- **Hell's Kitchen was the top-performing location** ($236,511), narrowly ahead of Astoria ($232,244) and Lower Manhattan ($230,057) — a fairly even 3-way split
- **Coffee is the dominant category** at $269,952 (39% of total revenue), followed by Tea ($196,406) and Bakery ($82,316)
- **Sales peak sharply between 8-10 AM** ($82,700-$88,673/hour), consistent with a morning commuter rush, then level off to a steady $40,000-$46,000/hour through the afternoon before tapering off after 6 PM
- **Revenue is fairly even across weekdays** ($97,000-$102,000 per day), with no single day dramatically outperforming others
- **Revenue grew steadily month over month**, from $81,678 in January to a peak of $166,486 in June — more than double over the 6-month window
- **Top single products by revenue** were Sustainably Grown Organic Lg ($21,152) and Dark Chocolate Lg ($21,006), while top products by quantity sold were Earl Grey Rg (4,708 units) and Morning Sunrise Chai Rg (4,643 units) — showing revenue leaders and volume leaders aren't always the same items
